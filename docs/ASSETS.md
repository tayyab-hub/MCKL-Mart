# Artwork provenance and branding review

Reviewed 14 October 2026. This is a record of sources and visual review, not a legal clearance or a claim of exclusive rights in AI output.

## Product illustrations

Muhammad Tayyab states that he generated all 17 images in `Products/` using AI for this individual academic project, and that they were not taken from Google or copied from another store. They are retained for coursework/portfolio presentation at his request. The original generation provider, model/version, prompts and applicable terms have not been supplied or independently verified.

All 17 images were visually reviewed. They depict fictional food, drinks and household products in project-branded packaging. No obvious unrelated store logo or identifiable customer was found. Some images contain invented packaging seals, nutrition or product claims; these are fictional illustration details, not verified certifications or claims about real merchandise. The images do not establish college approval.

The image files remain unchanged in this publication preparation. Their existing filenames are retained because both the seed and existing local database reference them. `ASSET_MANIFEST.json` records their SHA-256 hashes and dimensions for reproducible identification.

## Project identity

The college's [official website](https://mckl.edu.my/) was inspected on 14 October 2026. Its emblem uses a flame motif and college name. The previous MCKL Mart logo used a shopping-bag motif rather than that emblem. Both identities used blue/gold and the MCKL initials, so the storefront could still be mistaken for an affiliated service without context.

The standalone website logo was replaced with a new code-and-cart mark drawn directly as SVG during this portfolio preparation. It includes **ACADEMIC DEMO** in the wordmark and contains no copied college emblem, crest, motto or official typography artwork. The original blue/gold page design is preserved.

- Editable source: [`../assets/project-logo.svg`](../assets/project-logo.svg).
- Browser-compatible export: [`../Logo of the website.png`](../Logo%20of%20the%20website.png).
- Optional rendering script: [`../tools/render-logo.cjs`](../tools/render-logo.cjs), using Sharp. This is an authoring tool, not an application dependency.
- Text uses system font families; no font files are bundled in the logo source.

The product illustrations still contain their original fictional MCKL Mart packaging. They are not represented as images of actual college products. The site footer, homepage and README explicitly describe an unofficial academic demonstration. The college name identifies the coursework context only; no sponsorship, endorsement or operation is claimed.
